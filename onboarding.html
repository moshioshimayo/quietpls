Microphone

Microphone access is off.

quietpls needs the mic to monitor your voice level. Turn it on in System Settings, then come back here.

Turn on microphone access in System Settings:
  1. Choose Privacy & Security → Microphone
  2. Turn on quietpls

quietpls checks again when you come back.

Microphone

Microphone access is managed by your organization.

Your organization manages microphone access. Contact your administrator if you can’t enable quietpls in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Why this happens

A device management profile on this Mac controls which apps can use the microphone.
