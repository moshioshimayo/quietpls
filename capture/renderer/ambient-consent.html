Record beyond calls?

Always-on records your microphone and system audio even when you are not in a call.

  • Anyone near your microphone can be recorded. Tell them first and obtain any consent required where you are.
  • A persistent red ALWAYS ON indicator stays visible. Stop there or in the menubar at any time.
  • Speech is transcribed on this Mac in 10-minute chunks. There are gaps while each chunk is transcribed.
  • Transcripts are deleted after 24 hours by default. They live in ambient/, excluded from git history. Call transcripts keep their normal git history.
  • Audio exists temporarily while recording/transcribing and is deleted afterward, including on transcription failure.
  • Always-on does not restart automatically after quitting the app.

This is separate from “Ask when a call starts”.
