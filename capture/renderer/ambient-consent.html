Record beyond calls?

Always-on records your mic and system audio, including when you’re not on a call.

  • People near your mic may be recorded. Tell them first and get any consent required where you are.
  • The ALWAYS ON indicator stays visible. Stop recording there or in the menu bar.
  • Audio is processed on this Mac in 10-minute chunks. Recording pauses while each chunk is transcribed.
  • Always-on transcripts are deleted after 24 hours by default. They’re saved in ambient/, outside git history. Call transcripts keep their git history.
  • Audio is stored while recording and transcribing, then deleted—even if transcription fails.
  • Always-on stays off after you quit and reopen quietpls.

Always-on is separate from call prompts. The call app Never list doesn’t apply.
